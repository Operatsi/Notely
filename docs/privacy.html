Notely — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Developer: OperatSI

Application: Notely — Clean Notes & Productivity (com.operatsi.notely)

Our Promise: Notely is an ultra-clean, private, local-first note application. We do not collect your personal data, we do not sync your notes to the cloud, and we do not include third-party advertising SDKs.

1. Commitment to Confidentiality

OperatSI is dedicated to protecting user privacy and maintaining complete data confidentiality. Notely is engineered from the ground up as a private, local-first productivity tool. Your thoughts, notes, ideas, personal tasks, and schedules belong strictly to you and remain entirely under your control.

2. 100% On-Device Local Storage

All notes, to-dos, checklists, reminders, formatting preferences, and application settings are stored exclusively on your device's local sandboxed storage.

  • Notely does not transmit your notes or task descriptions to external cloud servers, remote databases, or third-party storage providers.
  • No user account or sign-in is required to use Notely.
  • Your data never leaves your hardware unless you explicitly choose to export or share it using Android's system share sheet.

3. Device Permissions & System Usage

Notely requests only the bare essential system permissions required to provide core functionality:

  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used solely to deliver reminder notifications and task alerts configured by you.
  • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Used exclusively to trigger precise reminder alerts at the date and time you set.
  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to reschedule active alarms after your device restarts so you never miss a reminder.

No permission is ever utilized for telemetry, user profiling, location tracking, or background monitoring.

4. Zero Data Collection, Sharing & Advertising

  • Zero Data Collection: OperatSI does not collect, harvest, aggregate, or store any personal data, identifiers, or usage analytics.
  • Zero Third-Party Advertising: Notely contains zero advertising SDKs, zero ad networks, and zero tracking pixels.
  • Zero Third-Party Analytics: We do not integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or any tracking libraries.
  • Zero Sale of Information: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to anyone.

5. User Rights & Permanent Data Deletion

You retain 100% ownership of everything you create in Notely:

  • When you delete a note or to-do item in Notely, it is immediately and permanently purged from your device.
  • Uninstalling the Notely application immediately removes all locally cached data and preferences from your device storage.

6. Security Architecture

Notely runs within Android's sandboxed security model, isolating its database and preferences from other applications installed on your device. Local data benefits from modern Android file-based hardware encryption (FBE).

7. Children's Privacy

Notely does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Because Notely is a local-only utility that does not collect any data, it complies with COPPA and global child privacy regulations.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

OperatSI may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect enhancements to Notely or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted directly within the application settings and on this live website.
